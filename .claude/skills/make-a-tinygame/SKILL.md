# Making a TinyGame cart

Follow `AGENTS.md` at the repository root: it has the examples to copy from, the
`./tg new` → `./tg check` → look at `out/<name>.png` → `./tg play` → `./tg send` loop, and what
makes a good cart. Read `docs/api-card.md` before writing any cart code, and use only what it lists.
