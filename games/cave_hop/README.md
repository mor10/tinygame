# CAVE HOP

**Made by:** TinyGame (sample game used by the engine's tests)

## How to play

- **Tilt** left or right, or turn the **main knob**, to run.
- **Click either knob** to jump. Hold it for a higher jump.
- Collect the coins and the gem. Do not touch the slime or the spikes.
- **Hold either knob:** pause menu.
